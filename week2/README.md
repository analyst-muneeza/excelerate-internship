## 📊 Week 2 — Eligibility & Enrollment Analysis

This week, I worked on a **deep-dive analysis of student eligibility and enrollment funnel** using multiple datasets.
The goal was to identify **conversion gaps, compliance risks, and actionable insights** to improve student enrollment outcomes.

---

### 🔍 Key Areas Covered

* Eligibility Funnel Analysis (Applicant → Visa Issued)
* Distribution Analysis (Admission, Deposit, I-20, SEVIS)
* Summary Statistics & Conversion Rates
* Comparative Analysis (Intake, Country, Engagement)
* Compliance & Data Quality Checks

---

### 📈 Key Insights

* ⚠️ **Major Drop-off in Deposit Stage**
  63% students are admitted, but only **28.6% pay deposit**
  → Biggest conversion gap in the funnel

* 🌍 **High-Risk Countries Identified**
  Nigeria, Pakistan, and Kenya show **low visa conversion rates**
  → Need targeted support

* 📉 **Low Engagement = High Dropout Risk**
  Students with only 1–2 interactions have just **18.3% deposit rate**

* 💰 **Funding Gap Issue**
  Undergraduate students face an average **$6,300 funding gap**

* 📊 **System Bottlenecks Identified**

  * I-20 delays across intake terms
  * 100 orphan SEVIS records (compliance risk)
  * Missing key fields like Admit_Date

---

### 💡 Business Recommendations

* 🚀 Launch **post-admission deposit campaigns**
* 📞 Re-engage low-interaction students
* 🌍 Provide **country-specific visa support**
* 💰 Introduce **financial aid triggers for funding gaps**
* ⚙️ Fix data issues for accurate tracking

---

### 🛠️ Tools & Skills Used

* SQL (Data Extraction & Analysis)
* Data Visualization
* Funnel & Cohort Analysis
* Business Insight Generation

---

### 📌 Outcome

This analysis highlights that while the **top-of-funnel is strong**, the **mid-funnel (deposit stage) is the biggest risk area**.
By focusing on engagement, financial support, and process improvements, conversion rates can be significantly improved.
